I designed, shipped, and scaled AchillesHR's internal advertising platform, which handles thousands of dollars of ad spend each month on behalf of our customers.

It manages budgets across several ad platforms through their APIs (Meta Ads, for example), and it can generate ad creative in one click using Google's Gemini 3 Pro Image model together with the Anthropic API.
