Sitelinks are the extra links that show up underneath a search ad. My team at PMG managed them for clients with a legacy internal app, and I led an engineering team through a complete redesign of it.

The new version uploads assets over 70% faster than the old one. Because it works through both the Google Ads and Microsoft Ads APIs, teams can make updates without having to care which platform an ad runs on.
